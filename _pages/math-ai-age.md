This page explains a study I am running. Explaining it is part of an AIRSHIP program to understand mathematics and science in the age of AI. I am a founding core member of <a href="https://airship-lab.github.io/">AIRSHIP Lab</a> at UIC, a community that brings together researchers across disciplines to explore AI in science.

AI is changing how mathematics is done, and I think this is a good moment to ask, carefully and with evidence, what it is that we value in our profession.

## The questions

- Which skills bring value to the mathematical profession? What kind of value do they bring, and for whom?
- Which of these skills will survive AI, which will change, and which can be made better?

## How I am going about it

The study reads published accounts by and about mathematicians (interviews, profiles, obituaries, memoirs and biographies) together with outside studies. Two AI models read each quote independently, every quote is checked against its source by a script, and the models' judgments are kept separate from the findings.

This is ongoing work, and the interactive page below is a draft for discussion.
