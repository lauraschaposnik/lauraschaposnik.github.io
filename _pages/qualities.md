An evidence-based study of what mathematicians, and those who write about them, say makes a good mathematician: more than 1,000 published interviews, profiles and biographies, from the fifth century to today. It is part of a study of the qualities that can be seen and nurtured at school, for the Math Talent Search I run with Equitech Futures, and of a larger study with AIRSHIP on the motivation and qualities of scientists at every stage of life and career.
