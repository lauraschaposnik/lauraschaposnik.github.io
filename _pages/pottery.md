I grew up watching my aunt Viviana, my father's sister, making beautiful art and beautiful statues and pottery. Early on during my childhood I'd spend afternoons in her studio doing some of her pottery and art classes with other students, and then having dinner all together at her home. After so many decades, I found myself wanting to learn something new - so in 2025 I started a wheel-throwing class here in Lincoln Park, Chicago.

<h2>Plates: My Everyday Dinnerware</h2>
<p>The plates are now my dinnerware, which makes me especially happy. Something that began as learning a new craft has become part of everyday meals. The blue surfaces, pale branching patterns, and little spots of colour give each plate its own character.</p>

<section class="pottery-section" id="mugs">
<h2>Mugs and Little Houses</h2>
<p>My mugs have become a small collection of little houses. I have been exploring carved outlines, different handle shapes, and blue and cream glazes. It is lovely to see how the same idea changes from one mug to another.</p>
