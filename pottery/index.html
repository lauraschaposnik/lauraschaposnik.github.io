Pottery

I grew up watching my aunt Viviana, my father’s sister, making beautiful art and beautiful statues and pottery. Early on during my childhood I’d spend afternoons in her studio doing some of her pottery and art classes with other students, and then having dinner all together at her home. After so many decades, I found myself wanting to learn something new - so in 2025 I started a wheel-throwing class here in Lincoln Park, Chicago.

Designing and Making a Berry Bowl

I wanted to make something useful for washing berries, and this bowl has become one of my favourite projects. It works wonderfully! The photographs below follow the design taking shape in clay, through the addition of the openings, to the finished glazed bowl.

Plates: My Everyday Dinnerware

The plates are now my dinnerware, which makes me especially happy. Something that began as learning a new craft has become part of everyday meals. The blue surfaces, pale branching patterns, and little spots of colour give each plate its own character.

Mugs and Little Houses

My mugs have become a small collection of little houses. I have been exploring carved outlines, different handle shapes, and blue and cream glazes. It is lovely to see how the same idea changes from one mug to another.

Bowls and Experiments in Form

The bowls give me room to explore different silhouettes and carry the little-house designs around a curved surface. I have also been trying more unusual forms, including two bowls joined by a shared handle.

How It All Started

I started my first wheel-throwing class around March 2025. These early photographs remind me of learning to make vases and bowls, then adding handles, engraving, and glazing. I like keeping them here alongside the newer pieces to see how things have developed.
